Safety

Photos

Critical items and NCRs only. Both stay open until you close them, and carry to the next day so nothing drops off. Shoot from the camera on a row and the photo lands below, captioned with the item and cleared when you close it.

Your data

Takes every booking, job, pre-pour item and done-log entry off the board and saves it to this device as plain files. Nothing is locked in here — open the CSVs in Excel, keep the JSON as the full record. Worth running before anything moves.

Pre-pour

Photos

Everything that has to be right before the concrete goes in — penetrations, starter bars, formwork, clean-out. Items carry to the next day until they are ticked off, and a photo shot on a row clears with it. Anything marked Hold the pour sits at the top.

Done log

Every labour job ticked off, newest first, kept by month. Jobs land here the moment the box is ticked and come back out if you untick one, so the log always matches the board.
